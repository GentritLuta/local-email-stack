A search is not one list. It is a stacked page, and you are on it or you are not.

When someone searches "best CPA in [city]" or "roof repair [zip]", five surfaces decide whether you get the call. Most owners obsess over one (their website) and lose on the other four.

02 · The one-hour win
Highest leverage

The Google Business Profile checklist

Almost always the fastest, cheapest win. Hold yourself to this standard. If you are missing three or more, that gap alone is likely keeping you out of the map pack, and it is fixable in an afternoon at zero cost.

Claimed & verified
Verification takes days. Start it today.
One precise primary category
+ every relevant secondary. A top ranking factor.
Every service listed individually
Each as its own line, not one vague paragraph.
20+ real photos
Team, trucks, before/after. Not stock. Add a few monthly.
Accurate hours & service area
Including holidays. Wrong hours send buyers away.
A Google Post in the last 30 days
Plus a seeded Q&A. Most competitors skip both.
The benchmark
A complete profile: verified, one precise primary category, every service listed, 20+ real photos, accurate hours and area, and at least one Post in the last 30 days. Six boxes. Hit all six before you spend a dollar on ads.

03 · What wins the click
Reviews

You can rank and still lose the call

Reviews do two jobs: they help you rank, and they decide who gets picked once you appear. Volume and recency beat a slightly higher rating with almost no reviews.

WINS THE CLICK
4.7★
180 reviews
Volume signals trust. Recent reviews signal you are still active.
vs
LOSES
4.9★
11 reviews
A near-perfect rating no one trusts because there is no volume behind it.

Your three benchmarks

Rating · hold 4.5★ or above
Below ~4.0, a large share of buyers filter you out before clicking.
Volume · match or beat your top-3 map-pack rivals
Open the map pack, count their review numbers, target the highest. For many trades that is 50 to 300.
Recency · several fresh reviews every month
A profile whose newest review is 14 months old reads as a business that slowed down.
