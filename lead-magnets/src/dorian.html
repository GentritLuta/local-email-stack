01 · Read it with your analytics open
How to use this

Open your own numbers next to it

Each leak gives you one number to pull, the benchmark it should hit, and the change that closes it. Most founders find their biggest leak in the first two sections, the hook and the gap right after the ask.

3s
The first three seconds decide whether the rest is even seen.
On short form, a view is counted almost instantly. The number that matters is how many are still watching at the 3-second mark and how far the average viewer gets. Fix the hook and every later number moves with it.
1

The hook, where you lose most people in the first three seconds

The check
Your 3-second view rate and average view duration (or average percentage viewed).
Healthy
Most viewers clear 3 seconds and the average viewer gets well past the opening, no cliff in the first few seconds.
The fix
The first frame and first line have one job: earn the next 3 seconds. Open on the result, the tension, or a number, never a slow intro or your logo.
2

Retention and trust, where attention without belief never converts

The check
Your retention curve and engagement (saves, comments, shares), not just views.
Healthy
A smooth retention curve with no mid-video cliff, and saves or comments that show people believe you, not just watch.
The fix
Earn belief with specifics and proof: a real number, a named mechanism, a concrete example. Vague authority gets attention but never the sale.

02 · The ask, the list, the speed
The three that actually book the call

Where the pipeline is really lost

3

The CTA, where a vague ask gets a vague response

The check
Count the next steps you offer in one piece of content. Is there exactly one, and is it specific?
Healthy
One clear, low-friction ask per piece. "Comment WORD", "DM me X", one link, not three.
The fix
Pick a single named action and make it effortless. A confused viewer does nothing. One ask, one word, one tap.
4

Lead capture, where you are renting your audience instead of owning it

The check
How many emails or direct contacts did you add this month, versus followers?
Healthy
A list you own (email or a captured DM funnel) that grows every week, independent of the platform.
The fix
Put a give-first lead magnet behind the CTA that moves people off-platform onto a list you own. Followers are rented, a list is yours.
5

Follow-up, the booked call you never made because you were slow

The check
Your time-to-first-touch after someone raises a hand, and how many touches follow.
Healthy
First touch in minutes, automated, then a multi-day sequence, not one message and silence.
The fix
Reply instantly when they bite, then run a short sequence over several days. Most calls are made in the follow-up, not the first reply.
