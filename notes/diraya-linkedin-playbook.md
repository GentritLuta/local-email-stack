# Diraya — LinkedIn Outreach Playbook

**Why this channel.** We proved Diraya's ICP (seed–Series-B SaaS/health-tech/fintech
founders + CTOs) is *un-scrapeable by email* — they hide their addresses. But the
exact same people are **fully reachable on LinkedIn**: their profiles are public,
and so are the buying signals that matter (new funding, a new CTO hire, an AI
roadmap post, an AI/ML hiring spree). LinkedIn is where the un-scrapeable becomes
reachable.

**The two-channel design (this is the whole point):**

```
  LinkedIn  =  FIRST TOUCH   (find + open the founder — where they actually are)
     |
     v   founder shares email  /  books Calendly  /  replies GHOSTS|REVIEW
     |
  EMAIL     =  NURTURE         (the 6 verified Diraya senders + 7-email sequence)
```

The email infrastructure we built (verified senders, 7-email Hormozi sequence,
GHOSTS/REVIEW lead magnets, Calendly) is **not wasted** — it moves from a failed
cold-scrape role into its correct role: multi-touch nurture of *warm* LinkedIn
leads. That is a stronger system than cold email ever was for this ICP.

## 1. Targeting — LinkedIn Sales Navigator filters

Build a Sales Navigator **Lead** search (not Account) with:

| Filter | Value |
|---|---|
| Job title | Founder, Co-Founder, CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product, Head of AI/ML, VP Product |
| Company headcount | 11–200 (proxy for seed–Series B) |
| Industry | Software Development, Internet, Financial Services, Hospital & Health Care |
| Geography | United States (primary; Diraya works any timezone) |
| Keywords | AI, ML, LLM, RAG, agent, machine learning |
| Seniority | Owner, Partner, CXO, VP, Director |

Then **layer a buying signal** (this is what makes the message land — Hormozi's
"right person at the right time"):

- **"Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days"** + keyword *RAG / agent / hallucination /
  eval / LLM in production* → they are publicly wrestling with the exact problem
  Diraya fixes. Highest-intent list.
- **"Changed jobs in past 90 days"** on CTO/VP Eng titles → new technical leader,
  building a roadmap, open to vendors.
- **Account filter "Hiring on LinkedIn" → AI/ML Engineer** → they need AI built and
  are paying to hire for it. Diraya is the faster alternative to a 4-month hire.
- **Recently funded** (cross-reference: Sales Nav "headcount growth >10%", or scan
  Crunchbase/TechCrunch funding feeds, or the LinkedIn "Funding" tab on the company
  page) → fresh budget, board pressure to ship AI.

Save 3–4 of these as **separate saved searches** so each gets a tailored opener
(see `{ai_signal}` slots in the DMs below).

## 2. The motion (mirrors the 7-email sequence, LinkedIn-native)

LinkedIn DMs are short, no subject lines, conversational. Same give-first Hormozi
logic, compressed. **Engage before you pitch.**

| Day | Step | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Warm** | Like + leave one genuine comment on their recent post. No DM yet. |
| 0 | **Connect** | Send connection request **with note** (DM-0 below, <300 chars). |
| on accept | **Open** | DM-1: soft, no pitch, founder-to-founder. |
| +2 | **Offer** | DM-2: the 8-week / eval-suite / 14-day-or-free offer. |
| +4 | **Proof** | DM-3: the one number (0.4% / 300-case eval). |
| +7 | **Give** | DM-4: GHOSTS list, no call. |
| +11 | **Decide** | DM-5: clean yes/no + REVIEW offer. |
| +18 | **Break up** | DM-6: close the file, give stays open. |

If they reply at any point → **stop the sequence**, talk like a human, push toward
the Calendly (`calendly.com/amoura-ma-diraya/30min`) or capture their email.

## 3. DM templates (Mohammed's voice: founder-to-founder, concrete numbers, no
em-dashes, no emojis, no exclamation marks, give-first)

**DM-0 — connection note (<300 chars).** Vary the first line by saved-search signal:

> Hi {first_name}, I run engineering at Diraya. We help seed-to-Series-B teams ship
> a production AI feature in 8 weeks with a written eval suite. Saw {ai_signal} and
> wanted to connect with people building in this space. No pitch.

`{ai_signal}` examples by list:
- posted-about-RAG → "your post on RAG reliability"
- new-CTO → "you recently stepped into the CTO seat at {company}"
- hiring-AI → "{company} is hiring on the AI side"
- funded → "{company}'s recent raise"

**DM-1 — open (on accept):**

> Thanks for connecting, {first_name}.
>
> Quick context, no pitch: at Diraya we get seed-to-Series-B teams from a brittle AI
> demo to a feature that passes a written eval suite in production, in about 8 weeks.
> Last fintech we did went from a demo to a 300-case eval suite in that window and
> kept shipping on top of it.
>
> If {company} has an AI feature that needs to actually land in production, happy to
> share how we would scope it. If not, good to be connected either way.

**DM-2 — offer (+2):**

> One more on this, then I will leave it.
>
> The shape for {company}: week 1 we write the eval suite together, week 2 the first
> milestone ships, weeks 3 to 8 we build and harden the agent against the evals. Day
> 56 you own the repo. Price is fixed before we start. First milestone lands day 14
> or you owe nothing and keep the code.
>
> Worth 15 minutes to scope it? calendly.com/amoura-ma-diraya/30min

**DM-3 — proof (+4):**

> One number: 0.4 percent.
>
> That is where a health-tech agent we built landed its hallucination rate after 8
> weeks. Started at 12 percent. Their support team stopped manually checking outputs.
> The lever was building the eval suite first, then the agent. Most teams skip that
> and that is why agents demo well and break in production.
>
> Want me to look at what your eval suite would need to cover for {company}?

**DM-4 — give (+7):**

> Switching to give-only, whether we ever work together or not.
>
> We keep a list called the Agent Ghost-Cases List: 40 production-killer edge cases
> most teams miss when shipping their first agent. No call attached. Want me to send
> it?

(When they say yes → send the GHOSTS PDF/link in-thread, or get their email and the
email system auto-fulfills.)

**DM-5 — decide (+11):**

> Clean question, {first_name}.
>
> If Diraya could ship a production AI feature for {company} in 8 weeks with a written
> eval suite and full handover, first milestone day 14 or you walk with no invoice,
> would you take a 15-minute call? A clear yes or no is the right answer either way.

**DM-6 — break up (+18):**

> Closing your file on my side, {first_name}. We take a small number of new builds
> per quarter and slots are filling.
>
> If the timing was wrong, tell me the right window and I reach back then. If nothing
> fit, no hard feelings. The Ghost-Cases List and a free architecture review for
> {company} stay open any time. Good luck with what you are building.

## 4. Daily limits (protect the account)

LinkedIn bans aggressive automation. Stay human-rate:

- **Connection requests: 15–20 per day max** per account (newer accounts: 10).
- **DMs: 20–30 per day** to existing connections.
- Spread across the workday, not in a burst.
- Personalize the connection note every time (generic notes tank accept rates and
  trip spam detection).
- Expected accept rate with a signal-based note: 25–40%. Of those, 5–15% reply.

At 20 connects/day × 20 workdays = 400 requests/month → ~120 accepts → ~10–18
conversations → a handful of calls. That is a healthy top-of-funnel for a
high-ticket build offer.

## 5. Tooling options

| Option | What it is | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Manual + Sales Navigator** | You/VA send connects + DMs by hand | Safest, ToS-clean, slow. Best to start. |
| **HeyReach / Expandi / Dripify** | Cloud LinkedIn automation, sequences the DMs | Faster, but ToS-gray; use conservative limits + a dedicated account. HeyReach is the most safety-tuned. |
| **Sales Nav + a CSV** | Export matched leads, track in a sheet | Pairs with either of the above. |

Recommendation: **start manual** to validate the message (first 2 weeks), then move
the winning DM sequence into HeyReach at conservative limits if you want volume.

## 6. How LinkedIn leads feed the email system

The verified Diraya senders + 7-email sequence become the **nurture + fulfillment**
engine for warm LinkedIn leads. Three capture paths:

1. **They book Calendly** → that is the win. Mohammed takes the call. Nothing else
   needed.
2. **They share their email** ("send me details") → drop into a CSV and run:
   ```
   py scripts/import-prospects-csv.py diraya their_leads.csv --niche saas_founder
   ```
   They land in the `diraya` pool and the 7-email sequence nurtures them (now a warm,
   opted-in contact, not a cold scrape). This is exactly what the email infra is for.
3. **They reply GHOSTS / REVIEW** → send the magnet in-thread, or capture the email
   and the existing auto-fulfill handles it.

**Do not** cold-email anyone who has not opted in via LinkedIn. The email channel is
for people who raised a hand. That keeps the verified senders clean and the whole
two-channel system compliant.

## 7. First-week checklist

- [ ] Confirm Diraya has Sales Navigator (or start the 1-month free trial).
- [ ] Build the 3–4 signal-based saved searches in section 1.
- [ ] Have the GHOSTS list + REVIEW one-pager ready as a shareable PDF/link.
- [ ] Send 10 connects/day manually with the DM-0 note for week 1 (warm-up the account).
- [ ] Track replies in a simple sheet; when someone converts, capture email → import.
- [ ] After week 1, review accept + reply rates, tune the opener, then scale to 20/day.
