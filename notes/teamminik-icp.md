## Two audiences (per user request: recruit agents + win sellers/buyers)

### Audience A — Real estate agents (PRIMARY: recruitment)
| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Licensed real estate agents, new and experienced |
| **Pain** | Inconsistent leads, high desk/monthly fees, low splits, no mentorship, no equity |
| **Dream** | Consistent deal flow + 85/15 split + equity, no upfront fees |
| **Buyer roles** | The agent themselves; also aspiring team builders |
| **Geo** | US, 22+ states, expanding nationwide |

Buying signals: agent recently licensed; agent posting about lead-gen struggles;
agent at a high-fee brokerage; agent switching brokerages (license transfer);
top producers feeling capped.

### Audience B — Home sellers / buyers (SECONDARY: listings & clients)
| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Homeowners considering selling; buyers entering the market |
| **Pain** | Pricing uncertainty, slow sales, untrustworthy agents |
| **Dream** | Top-dollar sale / right home, handled by a proven top-1% team |
| **Geo** | Arizona (primary), then the 22+ states the team covers |

Buying signals: expired listing; FSBO; relocation; life event (new job, growing
family); recent home-value lookups.

## Messaging angles
- **Agents:** dream outcome (predictable income + ownership), low risk (no fees,
  keep your license clean), proof (Michelle's numbers + media), urgency (cap
  resets, market window).
- **Sellers/buyers:** dream outcome (top-dollar / right home fast), perceived
  likelihood (top-1%, $354M volume, media-featured), low effort (team handles
  everything), low risk (proven track record).
