# Daily routine for the secretary

The recurring work this employee runs each shift, in order.

- Read the op's latest generated reports, digests, and logs on disk; note what changed since the last shift.
- Read anything in the data-inbox the operator dropped.
- Build the prioritized day: the most important items first, each with a one-line why and the next step.
- List who and what is waiting on the operator, and flag anything overdue or slipping.
- Draft the replies and messages he is most likely to need, clearly marked as drafts.
- State plainly anything you could not see (live inbox, calendar) and where it would come from.
