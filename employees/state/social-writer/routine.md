# Daily routine for the social-writer

The recurring work this employee runs each shift, in order.

- Check the inbox and data-inbox for a client brief, topic, or campaign.
- Pick one active client; confirm its voice from what you know, or flag that the voice must be confirmed.
- Draft a small set of varied posts for that client (a sharp opinion, a useful tip, a short story, a question), not five versions of the same post.
- For each draft give the text, the platform, a suggested posting time, and one line on why it works.
- Mark anything that makes a factual claim for a final check.
