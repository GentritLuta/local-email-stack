## QS vs QL — what determines which regime Aureon is in

This is the single open question that changes the numbers, the form, and whether financial statements are required. It must be settled before 15 July.

**The rule (verified):** A taxpayer whose **gross annual income does not exceed EUR 30,000** is **not subject to the 10% profit CIT**. Instead they file **quarterly tax on gross receipts** — the presumptive / small-business regime (**QS**) ([PwC — Taxes on corporate income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kosovo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)):

- **3%** of gross quarterly income from **trade, transport, agriculture** or similar — **minimum EUR 37.50/quarter**.
- **9%** of gross quarterly income from **services, professional, vocational, entertainment** or similar — **minimum EUR 37.50/quarter**.
- **10%** of gross **rental** income.

A taxpayer with **turnover over EUR 30,000, or one that keeps books**, is assessed on **profit from financial statements** and pays **10% CIT** via quarterly advances (**QL**) plus the annual settlement (**CD**) ([PwC — Income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kosovo/corporate/income-determination)).

**So, what decides it for Aureon:**
- **Turnover.** Confirmed under EUR 30,000 → presumptive **QS** is the default path.
- **Activity type.** Aureon's work reads as **services/B2B** → the **9%** presumptive rate, not 3%. Needs confirming against the registered NACE/activity code.
- **Election.** A small taxpayer **may voluntarily opt into the 10% profit CIT (QL/CD)** — worthwhile only if deductible expenses are high enough that 10% of profit beats 9% of gross. **Once elected, the company must stay in the CIT system** ([PwC — Taxes on corporate income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kosovo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)).

> **FLAG FOR LICENSED KOSOVO ACCOUNTANT (before 15 Jul 2026):**
> (a) Confirm Aureon files **QS presumptive at 9%** (services), not QL — or whether ARBK/ATK registration already locked the company into the books-keeping/CIT track. An Sh.P.K. is often defaulted to bookkeeping regardless of turnover; this is the borderline point.
> (b) If presumptive QS: confirm 9% vs 3% from the registered activity code, and confirm whether an **annual CD + full financial statements** are still required under QS or only the four quarterly QS statements.
> (c) Run the **9%-of-gross vs 10%-of-profit** comparison once H1 figures are in, to decide whether electing CIT is cheaper — before the election becomes irreversible.
