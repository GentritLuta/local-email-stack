## Notes for you (not part of the message)

- **Why these four questions matter.** Kosovo's presumptive regime (3% on trade/transport/agriculture, 9% on services/professional, minimum EUR 37.50 per quarter) applies to taxpayers with gross annual income up to EUR 30,000. The open question is whether an **Sh.P.K. (a corporation)** may use it at all, or whether the presumptive 3%/9% is reserved for individual/natural-person businesses while every LLC must keep books and file the 10% CIT with the annual CD return. That is genuinely unsettled for your case and is exactly why point (1) leads. Source: [PwC Kosovo — Taxes on corporate income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kosovo/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income).
- **One field to fill before sending:** your registered **NACE / activity code** in question 2 (from the ARBK extract). If you don't have it handy, drop the ARBK extract in the data-inbox and I'll insert it.
- **Quarterly payment timing.** Presumptive quarterly payments are due within 15 days of each quarter's close, so Q2 2026 lands on **15 July 2026** — consistent with the deadline you named. The final settlement for a presumptive taxpayer is due **31 March** of the following year. I've left both in the message for the accountant to confirm rather than asserting them as settled.
