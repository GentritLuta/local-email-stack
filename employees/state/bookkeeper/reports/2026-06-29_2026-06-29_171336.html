Notes for you (not part of the message)

  • Why these four questions matter. Kosovo's presumptive regime (3% on trade/transport/agriculture, 9% on services/professional, minimum EUR 37.50 per quarter) applies to taxpayers with gross annual income up to EUR 30,000. The open question is whether an Sh.P.K. (a corporation) may use it at all, or whether the presumptive 3%/9% is reserved for individual/natural-person businesses while every LLC must keep books and file the 10% CIT with the annual CD return. That is genuinely unsettled for your case and is exactly why point (1) leads.
