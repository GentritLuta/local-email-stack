BOOKKEEPER DATA DROP FOLDER
===========================

Put Aureon Global's financial documents here. The bookkeeper reads everything
in this folder at the start of each shift and uses it to keep the books and
prepare filings. It only reads; it never changes or deletes your files.

To unblock the 15 July 2026 quarterly filing, drop these three first:

  1. ARBK registration extract + fiscal number
     (shows incorporation date, legal form, and activity/NACE code)
  2. ATK EDI / e-deklarimi filing-history export
     (or a note that you will submit yourself and just need the figures)
  3. Every invoice issued since incorporation
     (date, client, amount, currency - PDF, CSV, or a simple list is fine)

Also useful whenever you have them:
  - Bank statements for all company accounts since incorporation (PDF or CSV)
  - Expense / supplier invoices and receipts
  - Any contract for rent, interest, royalties, or a non-resident provider

Accepted formats: PDF, CSV, XLSX, TXT, images of documents, or plain notes.
You can also just type figures into a .txt file here if that is easier.

Nothing here is sent anywhere. The bookkeeper prepares; you approve and submit.
