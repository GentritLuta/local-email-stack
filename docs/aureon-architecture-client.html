Variables pulled per account

  • First name, role, tenure in role (LinkedIn)
  • Company name, funding stage, last round date
  • Sales team size and 90 day hiring delta
  • Recent CRO or VP Sales hire
  • Public ramp time complaints or churn signals
  • Methodology stack currently in use (MEDDIC, BANT, Force, etc.)
  • One specific public post or quote we can mirror
Honesty rule. If a variable is missing or unverified, the line is dropped, not faked. The buyer never reads a sentence that does not pass smell test.

Why your emails reach the inbox

Reputation defended on six fronts.

Every safeguard automatic, every signal monitored. We never let a bad batch poison the pool.

Your domain, ten subdomains

Ten sending subdomains rotate the load. No single subdomain ever spikes. Reputation built on your name.

Recipient local hours

Every email lands 8 AM to 5 PM in the local timezone of the prospect, Monday to Friday. Never at 3 AM. Never on Sunday.

Snowball warmup

15 per day per subdomain in Week 1, ramping to 50 by Week 4. Full pool: 500 per day at steady state. Weekdays only.

Bounce kill switch

Any subdomain over 3% bounce in 24 hours gets auto paused. Alert lands in your inbox within the hour.

Suppression discipline

Every bounce, complaint, unsubscribe and reply auto suppresses. We never email the same buyer twice if they said no.

RFC 8058 compliance

Gmail and Yahoo 2024 bulk sender rules baked in. One click unsubscribe in every email. SPF, DKIM, DMARC aligned.
