# Mark-eting Campaign Setup

Prepared for Mark Eizema, Mark-eting B.V. Sending domain: getmark-eting.com. Website: mark-eting.co.

## The offer

SEO and online visibility, managed end to end, so US service businesses get found on Google by the buyers already searching for them. Every sequence opens with a free written visibility teardown as the primary ask. The call is always the secondary option, never the cold pitch.

## Who we target

Two segments, established US businesses with roughly $2M+ revenue, all states.

1. Trades and specialized services: plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, general contractors, restoration, pest control.
2. Professional services: law (personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration), accounting and CPA firms, dental practices, medspas, chiropractic and physical therapy, insurance agencies, financial advisors, landscaping.

Best converting titles: Owner, Founder, CEO, General Manager, Managing Partner, VP or Director of Marketing, CMO. Buying signals are layered where detectable: a new website with no inbound leads, a recent marketing hire, an ended SEO contract, Google Ads running with no organic strategy, an unclaimed Google Business Profile, or a site that has not been updated in years.

One clear ask per email. Founder-to-owner voice, no hype, no fabricated case studies. The free teardown carries the proof. Full rendered emails are in the separate sequence PDF.

## Sending setup

- 12 sending subdomains on getmark-eting.com, 12 rotating personas.
- Recipient-local 08:00 to 17:00, weekdays only.
- Warmup ramp 15 to 50 emails per domain per day, so the new domain warms safely.
- One-click unsubscribe and a full legal footer on every email.

## Lead sourcing

US service-business websites (team and contact pages), every address verified before it can be sent to. A compliance gate automatically skips any site that states it does not accept marketing or unsolicited email. The lead pool builds on its own every day.

## Replies and booking

Positive replies route to your Google Calendar booking link and to mark@mark-eting.co. Warm replies that ask to talk can be answered automatically with the booking link.

## Reporting

A daily campaign report is sent to mark@mark-eting.co and info@aureonglobal.de: emails sent, delivered, open rate, reply rate, positive replies, and calls booked.
