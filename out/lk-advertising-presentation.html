The engine

A fully managed cold-outreach system, end to end.

1

Sourcing

We auto-discover US real-estate brokerage team pages and extract named agents with verified, deliverable email addresses. The list rebuilds itself daily.

2

Verification

Every address is syntax, MX, SMTP and catch-all checked before it is allowed into a campaign. Only verified contacts get emailed, which is why bounce stays low.

3

Sending + warm-up

12 dedicated sending subdomains, each warmed and capped to protect reputation, for a combined throughput of up to 600 cold emails a day. A 7-step sequence per prospect.

4

Reply handling

Positive replies are routed straight to your cal.com booking link. Auto-replies and unsubscribes are classified and suppressed automatically.

The plan to scale it

Three levers, in priority order.

  • Lift reply rate from 0.5% toward 1%+ — sharper subject lines, market-specific first lines, and A/B on the 7-step sequence. This is the single biggest multiplier.
  • Grow verified volume — the sourcing engine adds fresh brokerage seeds daily; more verified contacts means more booked calls at the same rates.
  • Move sending onto lk-advertising.site — your own verified domain (DNS in progress) for full brand control and an independent sender reputation.
